Fancy a Pilsner?

Even if Pilsner is not your thing, Pilsen is the place to be. We will meet in the heart of the city, where history and innovation come together. Join us for the next edition of PyCon CZ, the largest Czech Python event!

What to expect?

You can look forward to inspiring lectures, practical workshops, and unforgettable connections with friends from the community.

Follow this website or our social media channels to be the first to know about the venue, program, speakers, and tickets!
