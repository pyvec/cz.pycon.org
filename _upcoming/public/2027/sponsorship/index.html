Become our sponsor

Value for your company

Sponsoring PyCon CZ is a great way to

  1. Support Python and the open source ecosystem your company relies on.
  2. Recruit developers, data analysts and testers: talk to attendees looking for their next job.
  3. Show your current employees you care about their favourite language and even make the event into an informal team-building experience for them.
  4. Help your employees widen their skills and knowledge.
  5. Promote your product: attendees are curious people looking for new tools and ideas.
  6. Find collaborators and partners for future development.

Event summary

  • 16–18 April 2027 in Pilsen
  • 400 attendees: professionals, students and hobbyist Pythonistas
  • 2 conference days with 2 tracks primarily in English covering topics like data processing, AI/ML, web-dev, cybersecurity
  • 1 day of workshops and sprints
  • 40+ diverse, local and international speakers
  • Recorded talks available to watch later for everyone
  • Broad range of both technical and community topics
  • Sponsor booths (Saturday & Sunday)
  • Social events and an after party
  • Organised by a community of volunteers
  • Friendly and inclusive environment
  • Food, coffee and tea provided on all 3 conference days

Sponsorship packages

We are not VAT payers.
Prices in EUR are for indicative purposes only.

Bronze

25,000 CZK / 1,024 EUR

This package includes:

  • 2 tickets for the conference
  • Logo on the conference website
  • Logo on the intermission slide deck shown during breaks
  • Partner announced on the PyCon CZ official social media channels

Silver

50,000 CZK / 2,048 EUR

Everything in Bronze plus:

  • 1 more ticket to the total of 3
  • Roll-up at the venue
  • Welcome package insert / promotions for attendees
  • One promotional post retweet / reshare on PyCon CZ official social media channels
  • Standard booth at the venue available as an option (see options below)

Gold

100,000 CZK / 4,096 EUR

Everything in Silver plus:

  • 1 more ticket to the total of 4
  • One more welcome package insert to the total of 2
  • One more promotional post retweet / reshare social media to the total of 2

Platinum

200,000 CZK / 8,192 EUR

Everything in Gold plus:

  • A large booth at the venue included in the price of the package
  • 6 more tickets to the total of 10
  • One more welcome package insert to the total of 3
  • One more promotional post retweet / reshare social media to the total of 3

Custom options (add‑ons)

Sponsored workshop

50,000 CZK / 2,048 EUR

Exclusive:
only two available

  • Unique opportunity to showcase your technology to the audience hands on
  • 60 - 90 minute slot during the main conference, marked as sponsored
  • Includes a ticket and optional coaching for the speaker
  • Attendance at the sponsored workshop is not guaranteed
  • Available with Platinum / Gold only

Venue Booth

20,000 CZK / 840 EUR

Booth at the venue
(max. 1.5 m × 2 m)

  • Includes 3 full tickets to the conference (for booth staff or for use at your discretion)
  • Available with Gold / Silver only (Platinum has a larger booth included)

Lanyard

50,000 CZK / 2,048 EUR

Exclusive:
only one available

  • Partner logo on the lanyard that everybody will be wearing
  • Lanyards will have only one Partner logo printed + logo of PyCon CZ
  • Available with Platinum / Gold only

Childcare

10,000 CZK / 420 EUR

Exclusive:
only one available

  • You will help us to acquire professional childcare providers
  • Every information (website, newsletter, on-site) about Childcare will include partner’s name

Coffee

75,000 CZK / 3,000 EUR

Exclusive:
only one available

  • Helping to cover the cost of the coffee at PyCon CZ
  • Coffee booth branded with partner’s logo (e.g. roll-up or banner that the partner provides)
  • Option to brand cups (at partner’s cost)

Speakers Diner

60,000 CZK / 2,500 EUR

Exclusive:
only one available

  • Sponsoring the cost of meal and drinks at the dinner
  • Partner’s logo on information posters during the dinner

Afterparty Drinks

60,000 CZK / 2,500 EUR

Exclusive:
only one available

  • Sponsoring the cost of drinks (soft drinks, beer and wine) at the afterparty
  • Partner’s logo on information posters during the party
  • Option to brand cups (at partner’s cost)

Custom sponsorship

from 10,000 CZK / 420 EUR

A few ideas:

  • Ice cream cart sponsor
  • Host a quiz or competition or donate prizes
  • Diversity sponsor – pay for free tickets for a target demographic
  • Event sponsor (board game night, workshop that requires specialised hardware, etc.)
  • Your idea! We’re open to anything interesting and fun…

Non-financial sponsorship

Your sponsorship doesn’t have to be financial: you can provide a service or goods directly to PyCon CZ attendees and get extra visibility.
